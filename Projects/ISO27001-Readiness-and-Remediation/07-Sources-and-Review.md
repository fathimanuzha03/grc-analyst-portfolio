# Primary sources and review record
**Project:** HarbourRoute simulation | **Version:** 2.0 | **Date:** 18 November 2026

## Scenario and analysis basis
HarbourRoute's business, commercial opportunity, control conditions, evidence records, scores and budgets were constructed for this portfolio. The scenario concerns logistics integrations, tenant boundaries and recovery commitments. It is not a report about a real company.

This release replaces the previous scenario-based version. Earlier versions remain in normal Git history; they are not evidence of current simulated conditions.
