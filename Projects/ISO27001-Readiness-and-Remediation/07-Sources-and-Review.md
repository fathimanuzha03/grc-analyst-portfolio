Public guidance checked on 18 November 2026. Control references are a focused cross-reference and must be checked against a licensed current standard before formal use. Source errors are not adopted: management review is Clause 9.3, not an Annex A “A.9.3” control; cloud outsourcing does not automatically exclude relevant physical/security requirements; certification is conditional on the assessor's decision.

## Reviewer checklist
- [ ] Sponsor agrees scope, decision deadline and customer acceptance criteria.
- [ ] Risk owners challenge likelihood, impact and target assumptions.
- [ ] Finance validates prices, staff allocations, contingency and stage releases.
- [ ] Legal validates processing roles and contractual/regulatory scope.
- [ ] Implementation lead completes full control selection and current-standard validation.
- [ ] Independent reviewer tests available real evidence and samples appropriately.
- [ ] Decision owner records approval, rejection or conditions; no signature pre-filled.
