# TechStart GRC Strategy

**Prepared by:** Fathima Nuzha M  
**Focus:** Compliance landscape, security assurance and governance  
**Business context:** UK SaaS company entering enterprise procurement  
**Document set:** Version 1.0 — substantive review pending

## Business problem

TechStart has 200 employees and processes personal data for approximately 85,000 users. Following an £18 million Series B, it is pursuing an enterprise financial services customer. The customer asks for ISO 27001, SOC 2 and GDPR assurance, while TechStart has informal security governance, an outdated policy and no risk register or supplier assurance process.

## Analysis and recommendation

The analysis evaluates applicable obligations, distinguishes confirmed governance gaps from unverified controls, compares assurance options and proposes a proportionate operating model.

The recommended strategy is to pursue ISO 27001 as the first independent assurance target, address privacy obligations immediately in parallel, and consider SOC 2 when buyer requirements justify an additional engagement. The proposed roadmap connects these priorities to owners, resources and evidence.

## Key decisions

| Decision | Rationale | What could change it? |
|---|---|---|
| ISO first for independent assurance | Responds to the prospect's explicit question and establishes missing governance | A non-negotiable buyer requirement for SOC 2 |
| Privacy starts immediately | Relevant legal duties already apply | Processing scope can change specific duties, rather than postpone assessment |
| DPO appointment requires assessment | Processing characteristics determine the requirement; headcount alone is insufficient | Evidence of qualifying large-scale monitoring or sensitive-data processing |
| PCI exclusion remains provisional | The stated scenario assumes no payment role | Billing or integrations reveal a merchant or security-impacting service-provider role |
| Reuse control evidence | One programme can support several requirements | Additional auditor/customer criteria require further evidence |

## Scope and evidence limitations

TechStart is a fictional business scenario. The documents provide desk-based analysis and proposed recommendations. No live configuration inspection, contract inspection or control testing was performed. No certification, management approval or implementation outcome is claimed. Costs are planning estimates, and substantive review remains pending.

## Proposed success measures

Within 90 days, the proposed programme should produce an approved scope, owned risk register, privacy remediation plan, policy baseline, incident contacts and initial control records. Ongoing measures include overdue high risks, review completion, recovery-test results and customer acceptance of assurance evidence.
