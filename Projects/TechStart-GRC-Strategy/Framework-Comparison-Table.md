# Framework comparison — TechStart Inc.

**Date:** 3 November 2026 | **Version:** 1.0 | Fictional educational case study

This comparison evaluates five instruments across their purpose, scope, assurance model, cost assumptions and suitability for TechStart.

| Dimension | ISO/IEC 27001:2022 | NIST CSF 2.0 | SOC 2 | GDPR / UK GDPR | PCI DSS v4.0.1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Publisher / authority | ISO and IEC | US National Institute of Standards and Technology | AICPA | EU legislature; separate UK legal regime as amended | PCI Security Standards Council |
| Type: mandatory / voluntary | Voluntary; contract may require certification | Voluntary for this scenario; may support contractual requirements | Voluntary; report may be a procurement condition | Mandatory where territorial and processing scope applies | Industry standard enforced through payment arrangements; applicability depends on role |
| Primary purpose | Manage information security risks through an ISMS | Prioritise and communicate cybersecurity outcomes | Independent assurance over service organisation controls | Protect individuals in personal data processing | Protect payment account data and relevant environments |
| Who requires it | Buyers may request scoped certification; prospect specifically asks about it | Leadership or customers may request alignment/profile | Buyers needing independent service assurance, including US enterprises | Law and supervisory authorities; customer contracts also impose processor duties | Payment brands, acquirers and customers for in-scope services |
| Scope | Defined organisational ISMS, service and dependencies | Organisation-selected current/target cybersecurity outcomes | Defined service system and applicable Trust Services Categories | Relevant controller/processor activities involving personal data | Entities storing, processing or transmitting account data, or able to affect its security |
| Key components | ISMS requirements, risk assessment/treatment, Statement of Applicability, selected controls, audits and improvement | Core outcomes, Profiles and Tiers; Govern, Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, Recover | System description, management assertion, criteria and auditor opinion; Security plus selected additional categories | Lawful processing, transparency, rights, accountability, processor duties, security and transfers | 12 principal requirement groups including secure systems, access, monitoring, testing and policy |
| Certification / attestation? | Certification by independent certification body; ISO does not certify companies | No NIST CSF organisational certification | CPA attestation report; not certification. Type 1 at a date; Type 2 over a period | No universal certificate proving all obligations; approved Article 42 schemes have defined limits | Applicable validation route: SAQ/AOC or ROC/AOC; agree with acceptance entity |
| Typical cost: planning allowance | £90k–£150k first year for this scope, excluding permanent hires and major remediation | £5k–£15k allocated staff effort for a focused initial profile; publication is free | £40k–£80k incremental first Type 2 programme after shared controls; separately budget hires and major remediation | £20k–£40k first-year privacy work within the shared programme; fee and representative needs depend on facts | £0 allocated while out of scope; £5k–£30k preliminary reserve for a simple future merchant setup, not a complex service-provider assessment |
| Time to achieve | Target 9–12 months from current baseline; external assessment governs award | 2–6 weeks for an initial profile; continuous improvement follows | Plan 6–12 months after approval; includes readiness, an agreed evidence period and reporting | Duties apply now; triage in 30 days, priority remediation in 90 days, ongoing obligations | Scope decision within 30 days; future simple merchant implementation might take 1–3 months, complex scope longer |
| Best for TechStart | Primary independent credential and governance foundation | Executive reporting and outcome mapping | Later customer-led assurance expansion | Immediate lawful handling of UK/EU personal data | No current project on the stated scenario assumptions; revisit if payment role changes |
| Overlap with | Evidence reusable for SOC 2, NIST and privacy security measures | Maps programme outcomes to ISO/SOC controls | Access, change, supplier, incident and monitoring evidence shared with ISO | Security measures overlap with ISO/SOC; lawful basis and rights require dedicated privacy work | Security controls overlap with ISO/SOC but payment-specific scope and validation remain |

**Cost basis:** These are analyst planning estimates for this fictional case, not verified market averages, statutory prices or quotations. ISO's £90k–£150k envelope includes the £20k–£40k privacy work and shared control effort; do not add them together. NIST profile work should also be absorbed where possible. SOC 2 is a conditional incremental budget. All figures exclude VAT, new permanent salaries and major engineering remediation; obtain scoped supplier quotes before commitment. No single universal price or duration exists for these instruments.

**Applicability:** PCI DSS is provisionally not applicable because the scenario assumes TechStart does not process payment cards and has no payment-security role. The scenario does not describe subscription billing. Confirm there is no merchant or security-impacting service-provider role; outsourced checkout alone does not automatically remove every obligation. Version 4.0.1 supersedes the brief's v4.0 reference. [PCI SSC overview](https://www.pcisecuritystandards.org/standards/pci-dss/), [revision announcement](https://blog.pcisecuritystandards.org/just-published-pci-dss-v4-0-1).

**Synthesis:** ISO/IEC 27001 is TechStart's most strategically valuable first assurance framework because it answers the prospect's explicit certification question, establishes ownership and risk management missing from the current programme, and creates evidence reusable for later SOC 2 work. This recommendation is a business judgement for this case, subject to buyer confirmation. Mandatory privacy work begins immediately in parallel; it does not wait for certification.
