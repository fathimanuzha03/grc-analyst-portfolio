# Recommended framework priorities

**TechStart Inc. | 3 November 2026 | Version 1.0 | Public portfolio — fictional case study**

**Decision requested:** Approve ISO 27001 as the first assurance target, immediate privacy remediation, and a customer-demand gate for SOC 2. The order below ranks assurance investment; it does not defer legal duties.

## Priority 1 — ISO/IEC 27001:2022 implementation and certification

**Business justification:** The prospect specifically asks about ISO certification. An ISMS addresses TechStart's missing governance, policy and risk processes while providing a defined certification scope to discuss with buyers. Shared control evidence can later support SOC 2. Confirm whether the buyer requires an awarded certificate, accepts a funded plan, or needs a SOC 2 report as well; no credential automatically closes the deal.

**Timeline:** Start within 30 days. Build foundations in months 1–3, operate controls in months 4–6, conduct independent internal audit and management review in months 7–9, and target certification in months 10–12. The target depends on remediation and certification body availability.

**Estimated cost:** £90,000–£150,000 first-year shared programme allowance: £35k–£55k allocated existing staff time; £20k–£35k fractional leadership/readiness support; £10k–£20k tooling/training; £10k–£20k privacy specialist support; £15k–£20k internal audit/certification. Sum: £90k–£150k. This analyst estimate includes privacy activity and is not a quote; permanent hires, VAT and major technical remediation are additional.

**Dependencies:** CEO sponsorship, senior GRC lead, fractional CISO, committed engineering capacity, service/dependency scope, risk owners, operating records and an independent certification body. Include current applicable amendments when acquiring the standard. [ISO overview](https://www.iso.org/standard/27001)

## Priority 2 — GDPR / UK GDPR compliance: mandatory parallel track from day one

**Business justification:** TechStart already processes personal data. Privacy work protects customers and supports credible contracting regardless of the certification schedule. Confirm controller/processor roles and EU territorial scope rather than treating all EU user data identically.

**Timeline:** In the first 30 days, inventory data flows, confirm ICO fee status, assess DPO/EU representative requirements, and triage notices, contracts and transfers. By day 90, close urgent gaps or record an escalation and dated remediation plan; exercise rights, complaint and breach workflows. Compliance remains ongoing; 90 days is a management target, not a legal grace period.

**Estimated cost:** £20k–£40k allocated within the ISO programme envelope, combining specialist support and existing team effort. Do not add this again. ICO fees and any representative or DPO service must be scoped separately if not covered by supplier quotes.

**Dependencies:** Legal ownership; Engineering, HR and Marketing input; processing records; contracts; transfer documentation. A DPO decision requires a processing-based assessment and independence safeguards. [ICO DPO criteria](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/accountability-and-governance/guide-to-accountability-and-governance/data-protection-officers/), [EDPB scope guidance](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/our-work-tools/our-documents/guidelines/guidelines-32018-territorial-scope-gdpr-article-3-version_en)

## Priority 3 — SOC 2: conditional follow-on assurance

**Business justification:** Revisit when Sales can identify opportunities whose buyers require a SOC 2 report. Avoid duplicating audit work before there is an evidenced commercial need. Prioritise the categories and report type customers actually need.

**Timeline:** Make the go/no-go decision in month 9. If approved, target a first Type 2 report in months 15–18, depending on readiness and an auditor/customer-agreed observation period; for example, six months of operating evidence followed by reporting. A Type 1 may be considered earlier only if the buyer accepts its point-in-time assurance. These are planning choices, not universal minimum periods.

**Estimated cost:** £40k–£80k incremental programme allowance after shared controls, excluding permanent hires and major remediation; obtain a scoped CPA quote and approve separately.

**Dependencies:** Sales business case, stable control operation, defined system, appropriate criteria and an independent licensed CPA firm. Reusing ISO evidence does not replace the examination. [AICPA SOC resources](https://www.aicpa-cima.com/resources/landing/system-and-organization-controls-soc-suite-of-services)

NIST CSF 2.0 is a supporting profile/dashboard method within existing work, not a fourth assurance project. PCI DSS remains out of scope subject to payment-role confirmation.

## Key risks of inaction in the next 90 days

| Risk | Business consequence | Immediate response |
|---|---|---|
| No credible assurance response | Enterprise procurement stalls; Sales may overpromise controls or dates | Agree buyer criteria and use a reviewed evidence pack |
| Unverified privacy contracts/transfers | Contract disputes, complaints and possible regulatory action | Legal reviews data flows and urgent obligations |
| Unowned security risks | Remediation slips while access, incident or recovery weaknesses may persist | Assign owners, due dates and CEO escalation |
| No operating evidence | Certification slips because documents alone cannot demonstrate a functioning programme | Collect evidence from the first control cycle |

## Answer to the enterprise prospect

**Accurate response on the scenario facts:** “TechStart is not currently ISO 27001 certified and has not supplied a SOC 2 report. We have commissioned a GRC assessment recommending an ISO/IEC 27001:2022 programme. Implementation funding and the certification timetable are awaiting leadership approval. We can discuss your assurance requirements and share verified information about our current controls and privacy arrangements.”

**Use only after leadership approval and programme launch:** “TechStart is pursuing ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certification for the agreed SaaS service scope, targeting completion within 12 months, subject to readiness and independent assessment. We are addressing privacy obligations in parallel. We do not currently hold a SOC 2 report; we can discuss your required report type and scope.”

Sales must not claim GDPR compliance or an audit booking without supporting evidence. Jamie Brooks owns approval; Rachel Davies reviews privacy wording; Sarah Chen validates technical claims; Marcus Webb confirms buyer acceptance criteria.
