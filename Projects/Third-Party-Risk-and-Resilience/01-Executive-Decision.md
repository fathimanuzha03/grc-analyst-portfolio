# Executive decision brief
**Decision:** N-D01 | **Proposed sponsor:** Managing Director | **Status:** Proposed

## Recommended decisions
| Supplier | Decision proposed | Prerequisite | Business owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| FrameRelay | Hold external asset sharing | Authenticated reviewers, expiry, appropriate download permissions and complete access records | Production Director |
| ColdHarbor Archive | Do not make it the sole release-critical recovery path | Prove retrieval within approved objectives, independent key recovery and usable exports | Head of Post-production |
| Cutline Collective | Stop new footage transfers until safeguards are evidenced | Named accounts, protected endpoints/working areas and deletion/return control | Post-production Producer |
| ScreenBridge Delivery | Permit only reviewed scope after release conditions | Test alternate delivery route and identify shared dependencies | Delivery Manager |

Keep existing assets secure while making any transition. Do not delete the only recoverable master or abruptly disable delivery without a continuity decision.

## Business rationale
Northbank's release schedule depends on several suppliers handling unreleased intellectual property, identifiable footage and client access. A shared bearer link or an unmanaged edit copy can expose material before launch. An archive that returns assets too slowly can delay delivery even if it protects confidentiality well.

The synthetic archive exercise produces an **11-hour retrieval interval** against a proposed 8-hour RTO. The review-link sample has **2 unauthenticated, non-expiring links out of 5**. These are constructed exercise results, not actual incidents.

## Options considered
| Option | Planning envelope | Consequence | Decision |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Continue with supplier questionnaires alone | £3k–£5k | Leaves tenant configuration and recovery unproven | Reject |
| Enforce sharing/access gates and rehearse recovery/exit | £30k ceiling | Targets release exposure while retaining viable suppliers | Select |
| Replace every media supplier immediately | £70k–£100k before migration overrun | Adds disruption before alternatives are tested | Defer; re-cost only if gates fail |

All estimates are invented planning assumptions, not supplier quotations or promised savings.

## Resource request and milestones
£30,000 total: £13,000 allocated staff effort and £17,000 incremental reserve, including contingency. This excludes replacement subscriptions, bulk migrations, permanent hiring and VAT.

Day 7: contain review-link and external-editor exposure. Day 21: approve supplier terms and evidence expectations. Day 35: complete archive/alternate-delivery exercises before the six-week campaign deadline. Day 60: review open findings and decide transition or permitted exceptions.
