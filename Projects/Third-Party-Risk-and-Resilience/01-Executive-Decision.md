Use synthetic data for product evaluation until live-data gates are met. Conditional approval does not mean “upload now, fix later.” If legacy support cannot operate safely, escalate a controlled service-transition decision to the executive sponsor; avoid an unplanned shutdown that harms customers.

## Why this matters
DataBridge analyses confidential commercial and personal data for large clients. A trusted supplier can expose data, interrupt recovery or provide a privileged path into customer environments. Encryption and an assurance report do not settle processing instructions, incident cooperation or the customer's own responsibilities.

**Current model:** 8 suppliers, 4 High / 3 Moderate / 1 Low. No Critical score arises in the numerical model; legal/security hard stops can nevertheless block a supplier. Posture assumptions and scoring confidence are documented, not hidden behind a composite score.

## Options
| Approach | Benefit | Trade-off | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| Onboard from questionnaire scores alone | Fast procurement | Could approve a legally or operationally unacceptable dependency | Reject |
| Risk-tiered checks with mandatory gates | Focuses effort and defines usable approval conditions | Requires owners, supplier evidence and escalation discipline | Select |
| Replace every supplier without independent certification | Simple rule | Excludes potentially suitable suppliers and does not resolve contractual/operational exposure | Reject |

## Funding and delivery
Proposed 90-day envelope: **£45,000**, including £20,000 allocated internal effort and £25,000 external/tooling/testing reserve. No new permanent salaries, VAT, replacement subscriptions or migration project costs are included. These are portfolio estimates requiring quotes.

Day 10: restrict remote support and obtain data/access flows. Day 30: complete priority evidence and contract reviews; hold unresolved onboarding. Day 60: run restore and incident exercises. Day 90: executive review closes findings or approves dated exceptions where permitted.

## Authority and guardrails
Business owners own supplier risk; CISO recommends security decisions; Legal validates duties and terms; Procurement records conditions and prevents release before gates close. Executive sponsor approves High-risk exceptions with expiry and compensating controls. No exception can waive law, customer authorisation or a technical hard stop.
